# Third party notices

## Weather Icons

`src/shared.liquid` inlines glyphs from TRMNL's hosted icon pack at
`https://trmnl.com/images/plugins/weather/`. They are inlined, not linked,
because a URL the render worker cannot reach fails the whole screen.

> Weather Icons by Erik Flowers, <https://erikflowers.github.io/weather-icons/>.
